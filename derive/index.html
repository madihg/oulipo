dérive

A dérive is an unplanned drift through the city. The Situationists coined it; San Francisco keeps proving it works. Every Monday this page re-reads the city and lays out the week worth drifting through.

The dérive itself runs on a broadcast channel: one text when the week’s drift is set. Tap the button - your messages app opens with the join note prefilled. Send it and you’re on the list.
