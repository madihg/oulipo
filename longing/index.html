I wish you were

Ten small programs about wanting what is not here. They start with a lover and a beloved and end with a migrant and a home.

Each part takes one idea from Anne Carson’s Eros the Bittersweet or Plainwater, runs it as a program, and finishes the title with one sentence of mine. The two lines in quotation marks are Carson’s. Black is the one who wants. Pale grey is what is wanted.

Part 02 is a Python couplet of mine from 2026, and part 04 is a variation on another one. Their code is shown below the screen. The form follows bpNichol’s First Screening (1984), twelve kinetic poems he wrote in Apple BASIC.

There is no CSS on this page, only HTML and JavaScript. The numbered buttons switch parts. Click, hold, or move over the screen, and open the tip on each part. The arrow keys and the space bar work too.
