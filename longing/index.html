I wish you were

        

          Ten small programs about wanting what is not here. They start with a
          lover and a beloved and end with a migrant and a home.
        

        

          Each part takes one idea from Anne Carson’s Eros the Bittersweet
          or Plainwater and runs it as a program. The sentence above each
          program is my own, most of them from my notebooks. Black text is the
          one who wants. Blue text is what is wanted. Parts 02 and 04 are two
          Python couplets of mine from 2026, ported to this screen. Their code
          is shown under them. The form follows bpNichol’s First Screening
          (1984), twelve kinetic poems he wrote in Apple BASIC.
        

        

          The numbered buttons switch parts. Click, hold, or move over the
          text. The arrow keys and the space bar work too.
