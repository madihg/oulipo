I wish you were

Ten small programs about wanting what is not here. They start with a lover and a beloved and end with a migrant and a home.

Each part takes one idea from Anne Carson’s Eros the Bittersweet or Plainwater and runs it as a program. The sentence above each program is mine, most of them from my notebooks. The two in quotation marks are Carson’s. Black text is the one who wants. Blue text is what is wanted.

Part 02 is a Python couplet of mine from 2026, and part 04 is a variation on another one. Their code is shown above the screen. The form follows bpNichol’s First Screening (1984), twelve kinetic poems he wrote in Apple BASIC.

There is no CSS on this page, only HTML and JavaScript. The numbered buttons switch parts. Click, hold, or move over the screen, and open the tip on each part. The arrow keys and the space bar work too.
