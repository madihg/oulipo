NeuroScript Attributes: FactStatement 

 ?FactStatement

 The ?FactStatement attribute is set when someone states some
other type of fact. Usually this is just an assertion that something has happened,
or will happened, or is happening.

 Examples

"Jack ran across the yard",
"I ate that bird."
"I'll worry about that later."
"This dog fetches thrown sticks."
