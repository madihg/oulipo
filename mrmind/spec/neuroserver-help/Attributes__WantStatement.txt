NeuroScript Attributes: WantStatement 

 ?WantStatement

 The ?WantStatement attribute is a statement that someone
wants, needs, or desires something.

 Examples

"Uncle Sam needs you."
"The barn needs a new coat of paint."
"I want a bot of my very own."
