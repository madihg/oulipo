Always 

 The Always condition is a condition that is always true,
equivalent to IfHeard "*" Then . The Always condition
is most useful in Sequence topics and
 Otherwise conditions. Unlike other 
condition keywords, the Always condition is not preceded by the
keyword If or followed by the keyword Then .

 Always conditions cannot be combined with other conditions
using And or Or .

 Example:

// This topic might be used as the last topic in a script,
// to handle questions that aren't handled anywhere else
Default Topic "Last Topic" is
 Always
 Say "I'm sorry, I'm not sure I know what you mean";
 Done
EndTopic
