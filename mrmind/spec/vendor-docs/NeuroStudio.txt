 NeuroStudio TM features: 

 Integrated Personal Bot Server lets your bot 
chat with
visitors to your web site, present them with pictures and media clips, 
or navigate them
to new URL destinations. You can simultaneously chat with your bot via 
the built-in
'console' window.

 The Artificial Intelligence is 'under the hood' 
- no Ph.D. required!
The Gerbil compiler and runtime AI engine perform the difficult
chores behind the scenes so you don't have to program them. 

 Neuromedia�s GeRBiL (General Robot Building Language) 
technology can be used for any language, not just English. Because Gerbil is 
not dependent on English grammar, it can be used with a wide range of
languages "as is." 8-bit character sets are supported. 

 Information is organized in a common-sense way: 
topics, answers, things to say and do, and patterns to respond to
are organized the way you think. 

 Script-browsing editor- jump between the
definitions of related topics, patterns, and answers via 
point-and-click. 

 "Why did you say that?" navigation- when your
bot produces an unusual result, you can instantly find out why with
a click of the mouse. 

 Standard libraries for recognizing the most
common kinds of questions and statements are already written for you-
just include them in your bot. More are becoming available from
Neuromedia all the time - watch for announcements on our site
(Or write your own libraries and sell them to third parties!) 

 Evaluation tools help locate problems in
scripts and ensure that changes made to scripts will not cause new 
problems.

 Spell-checking at compile time AND runtime-
check your content for spelling errors, and build a dictionary that
corrects users' spelling at runtime, too. 

 Full working bot included - all the Gerbil
source files to Shallow Red, who responds to over a thousand topic
areas, are included along with documentation. 

 Intelligent script compiler provides error
messages that mean something, and helpful style tips, too. 

 Test tools let you check how your bot
responds to thousands of inputs with the click of a button. 

 Log analyzer - browse user inputs, and use them 
to create new topics in your bot, or improve old ones, quickly and
easily. You can even store them for later testing.
