Other Bots Made Using Neurostudio 

 Bots created with NeuroStudio

 Or, more accurately, some of the bots we know about...

 Julie-1 : Built by Govcon.com, the leading supplier of government
 contracting information, Julie-1 provides answers to service and
 paperwork questions for Govcon's clients. 

 Millie : Millie is a bot built by Y2klinks.com. Millie gives answers
 to people who have questions about computers and software
 relating to Year 2000 compliance. 

 Mr. Mind : Developed for a PBS project by Producer Peggy Weil and
 the WebLab Group, Mr. Mind puts a new twist on the classic
 Turing Test by asking you to prove to the computer that you
 are human. Good luck! 

 Shallow Red : Chat with "Shallow Red," our SalesBot built with
 NeuroStudio. Shallow Red can tell you all about bots and
 Neuromedia products, show you some useful urls, and can
 even play a little music... 

 BRAIN : Bill Orlowski's InfoBot knows all kinds of facts,
 and can help you find stuff. And like Bill, BRAIN
 has quite the affinity for parrots. 

 Tecia : An inquisitive chat hostess in the employ of
 Robots by Jack. Unlike SR and BRAIN, she doesn't
 like to fess up that she's a bot. You'll never
 be lonely again...
