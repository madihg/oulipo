?WhatUserSaidBefore 

 The ?WhatUserSaidBefore attribute refers to the second-most-recent
text input from the user. It is the exact text typed in by the user, not modified
by spell-checking or other operations. Most of the time you will wish
to check ?WhatUserMeantBefore 
 instead
of ?WhatUserSaidBefore .
 Logins and other "non-text" 
actions by the user do not change ?WhatUserSaidBefore .
