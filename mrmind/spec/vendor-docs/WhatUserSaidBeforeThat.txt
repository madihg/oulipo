?WhatUserSaidBeforeThat 

 The ?WhatUserSaidBeforeThat attribute refers to the third-most recent text input
from the user. It is the exact text typed in by the user, not modified
by spell-checking or other operations. Most of the time you will wish
to check ?WhatUserMeantBeforeThat 
 instead
of ?WhatUserSaidBeforeThat .
 Logins and other "non-text" 
actions by the user do not change ?WhatUserSaidBeforeThat .
