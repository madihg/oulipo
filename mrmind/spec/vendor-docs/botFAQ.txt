The Shallow Red FAQ 

 The Shallow Red FAQ

What is Shallow Red? 

Shallow Red is a marketing information "bot" created
 with the NeuroStudio authoring system. Shallow Red is primarily a
 Virtual Representative, providing answers to basic questions about Neuromedia,
 Neuromedia's products, and how bots are created and used. Red is also pretty good 
 with mundane stuff.

Is Shallow Red a search engine?

No, but bots make a good front end to search engines. For example, if you ask red to find information
 that's not in the script, you may just have your request referred to Alta Vista! Unlike search engines,
 Shallow Red does not rely on simple keywords. Instead, the order of words,
 and many common words that search engines ignore, can be important in
 helping Shallow Red understand what you are saying. So remember to ask
 your questions in plain English sentences. 

Is Shallow Red an "Eliza" clone? 

 No. Shallow Red does not feign understanding when the
 answer to a question is not known. Unlike early bots such as the well-known
 "Eliza the Psychiatrist" which are made to entertain, Shallow Red means
 business. Shallow Red is scripted to provide meaningful answers to
 questions within a specific domain for people who need them, and to do so in
 a polite and businesslike way. More whimsical agents can, however, easily be
 scripted with NeuroStudio.

What's the easiest way to make comments, talk to the botmaster, etc?

When chatting, any line that begins with // will be sent to the BotMaster as a
 comment on the previous interaction. Your comments are appreciated. 
 Send us some feedback- we'd like to know what you think. 

Can Shallow Red play relaxing Samba music? 

 Yes. And more importantly
 Shallow Red can turn it off, too. 

Is there anything else I should know? 

 Shallow Red is being modified and improved all the
 time. Be sure to check back periodically.
