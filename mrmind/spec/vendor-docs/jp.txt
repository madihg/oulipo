 Joseph P. Lanham 

 Chief Operating Officer

Joseph P. Lanham most recently served as Vice-President of sales and
marketing for the Human Element, Inc. (THEI) an interactive learning and
communications company. Mr Lanham led THEI sales and marketing efforts of
interactive development services and products to Fortune 500 companies and
major educational publishers such as Toyota, Procter and Gamble, Oracle,
Times Mirror and MacMillan.

In his four years at THEI, the organization grew from six people and one
office to twenty-seven employees and three offices across the US.

Prior to THEI, he played an intregal role at ComputerArtworks, one of the
countries first developers and reseller of interactive multimedia tools and
applications. While at ComputerArtworks he established a client base that
included IBM, Miller Brewing, Chiquita International and Spelling
Entertainment.

Mr. Lanham graduated from Boston College in 1981.
