 Scott Benson, Ph.D.

 Executive Vice President and CTO

Scott Benson was awarded the Ph.D. in Computer Science 
from Stanford University in 1996, where he worked with 
 Nils Nilsson 's
"BOTS" research group on Machine Learning 
and Intelligent Agents.
His dissertation is titled "Learning Action Models for
Reactive Autonomous Agents." He received the B.S. degree in 
Computer Science from Cornell University in 1991.
 Scott joined Neuromedia in 1995, working on various
consulting projects with Walter Tackett , and became a 
co-founder of Neuromedia's product-focused venture upon
its incorporation in 1996.
